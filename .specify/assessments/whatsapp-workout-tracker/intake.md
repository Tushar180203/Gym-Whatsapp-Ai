## Idea (as captured)

> Build a WhatsApp-first workout tracking product for people who already know how they train and already have a workout routine. Users configure their workout split, training frequency, exercises and preferences through a web app, then use WhatsApp during workouts to start sessions, log sets in natural language, correct sets, reorder exercises, add unknown/custom machines, query workout history and complete workouts. AI should understand user intent, unknown exercises and messy workout language, but deterministic backend logic must validate and persist workout state. The web dashboard should provide workout history, exercise progression, workout frequency, weekly sets and muscle-training frequency.

## Restated

The proposed product is a workout tracker centered on WhatsApp for in-workout logging, with a web app used to configure routines, exercises, preferences, and training structure. It should use AI to interpret messy workout messages and unknown exercise language, while deterministic backend logic validates and persists workout state and dashboard metrics.

## Origin & Context

- **Raised by**: Tushar Sharangpani, project owner.
- **Trigger**: The idea comes from the friction of traditional workout tracking during gym sessions. Users who already know their workout have to repeatedly open a workout app, navigate to exercises, enter sets/reps, and move between screens. The proposed opportunity is to make workout logging conversational through WhatsApp while using a web dashboard for setup and analytics.
- **Initial user segment**: Self-directed intermediate-to-experienced gym users who already have a workout routine or split and primarily need a faster, lower-friction way to log, retrieve, and analyze training data.
